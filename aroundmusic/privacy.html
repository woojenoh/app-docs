Around Music Privacy Policy

Around Music (the "App") shows what people nearby are listening to and shares what you're playing with them. This policy explains what information the App uses, who can see it, and how long it's kept.

Information we collect

  • Account. You sign in with Apple. We receive a unique account identifier from Apple and, if you choose to share it, your email address (which may be a private relay address). We don't receive your name.
  • Profile. The nickname you choose and your sharing settings (Share Location, Share Music, Share Listening History).
  • Location. While the App is open and you allow location access, it uses your device's location (requested at roughly 100 m accuracy) to find people nearby and to place you on their radar. We keep only your most recent location.
  • Music activity. While the App is open and Share Music is on, the Apple Music catalog ID of the song you're playing. Songs you play for at least 10 seconds are added to your listening history, together with an approximate location rounded to about 1 km, so they can be counted in Trending.
  • Bookmarks. Songs you save.
  • Safety. People you block and reports you submit, including the reason you choose.

What other people can see

  • Your nickname, the song you shared in the last 30 minutes, and your approximate distance from them, rounded to about 500 m. Your exact location and coordinates are never shown to anyone.
  • The songs you played in the last 3 days, on your profile, unless you turn off Share Listening History.
  • Trending shows only how many people played each song in an area, never who played it or where exactly.
  • People you block can't see you, and you can't see them.

How we use information

We use this information only to provide the App's features (the radar, Trending, profiles, bookmarks) and to keep the community safe, such as reviewing reports. We don't sell your information, show ads, or use it for tracking across apps or websites. The App doesn't use analytics or advertising services.

Service providers

Account and app data are stored with Supabase, which hosts the App's database and sign-in. Sign in with Apple and Apple Music are provided by Apple. Song titles and artwork are loaded from Apple Music.

How long we keep information

  • Listening history is deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Your location is removed when you turn off Share Location, turn off location access for the App, or sign out.
  • Your current song is removed when you turn off Share Music or sign out.
  • Everything else is kept until you delete your account.

Your choices

You can turn sharing on or off at any time in the App's Settings, change location or Apple Music access in iPhone Settings, and block or report other people from their profile. To delete your account, go to Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, bookmarks, listening history, blocks, reports, and account.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact
