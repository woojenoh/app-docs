Around Music Support

Around Music shows what people nearby are listening to on Apple Music and shares what you're playing with them. Find new songs, see what's trending near you, and save the ones you love.

Why don't I show up on other people's radar?

Your music is shared only while Around Music is open. iPhone doesn't let apps see what Apple Music is playing in the background, so keep the app open while you listen. Also check that Share Location and Share Music are turned on in the app's Settings, and that location access is allowed in iPhone Settings > Around Music > Location.

Why can't I see anyone nearby?

The radar shows people who shared a song in the last 30 minutes. If no one is around, try a wider Radar Range in Settings, or open Trending to see what's popular near you over the last 7 days.

Some songs I play aren't shared

Only songs available in the Apple Music catalog can be shared, because other people need to be able to find and play them. Songs that exist only in your library, such as uploaded tracks, aren't shared.

Is my exact location shared?

No. Other people never see your location on a map. They only see an approximate distance, rounded to about 500 m (a third of a mile). You can turn off Share Location in Settings at any time, and you'll disappear from everyone's radar.

Who can see my listening history?

People who open your profile can see the songs you played in the last 3 days. To keep your history private, turn off Share Listening History in Settings. Trending only shows how many people played a song, never who played it.

Make a Mix doesn't play

Playing songs requires an Apple Music subscription. Play Mix adds the songs to your Apple Music queue. Save as Playlist also needs Sync Library to be turned on in iPhone Settings > Apps > Music > Sync Library.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Delete Account in the app and confirm with your Apple ID. Your profile, bookmarks, listening history, and account are permanently deleted.

Safety

Report someone

Open the person's profile, tap the ••• button in the top-left corner, and choose Report. Pick a reason and we'll review the report and take action if it breaks our rules.

Block someone

Open the person's profile, tap •••, and choose Block. You won't see each other on the radar or in profiles. You can unblock people in Settings > Blocked Users.

Contact

For questions, feedback, or to report a problem, please email aroundmusic@potato.pizza. You can also use Settings > Contact Us in the app, or open an issue on GitHub.
